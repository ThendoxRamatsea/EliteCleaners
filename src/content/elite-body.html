Home cleaning

Two ways to book Elite Home Cleaning

Elite Standard is our full-service clean, including laundry and ironing. Elite Quick is a professional home clean without laundry — built for a faster visit.

Elite Standard

The comprehensive clean. Every room, plus a load of laundry and ironing included in the visit.

  • Full room-by-room cleaning
  • Laundry & ironing included
  • Interior appliance cleaning
vs

Elite Quick

A professional, efficient clean of every room — without laundry, ironing or deep appliance interiors.

  • Full room-by-room cleaning
  • No laundry or ironing
  • Faster visit, lighter scope

All Elite Standard and Elite Quick packages include cleaning products and equipment. Elite Estate and Elite Quick Estate homes (4+ bedrooms) require a custom quote.

About Elite Cleaners

More than clean.
Peace of mind.

At Elite Cleaners, we believe a beautifully clean space should come with something even more valuable: peace of mind. When you invite us into your home or workspace, you're trusting us with a space that matters to you — and we take that responsibility seriously.

Our goal is simple: an exceptional, reliable cleaning service, so you never have to wonder whether the job will be done properly. Our cleaners are carefully selected and trained to work according to the Elite Cleaners standard, with attention to detail, professionalism and respect for every space they enter.

Whether you live in a small apartment, a family home or a large estate, our commitment stays the same: to deliver quality you can see, freshness you can feel, and service you can trust. Because for us, cleaning isn't simply a list of tasks — it's giving you back your time, and creating that feeling when you walk through the door and think, "my home is clean."

Exceptional cleaning. Trusted service. Complete peace of mind.
