# Clarify Home Cleaning and Laundry Services

## Goal
Make both services immediately understandable while preserving the existing Clean Showcase design, navy-and-gold palette, typography, spacing, photography, and booking flow.

## Home Cleaning
- Rename the two service families everywhere customers see them:
  - **Elite Home Clean** — “Our complete home cleaning service.”
  - **Elite Quick Clean** — “A streamlined clean for homes that need a refresh.”
- Keep the existing bedroom-based prices and booking behaviour unchanged.
- Replace the brief “versus” introduction with an elegant two-column comparison that clearly marks shared essentials and the premium extras only included with Elite Home Clean.
- Cover bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchen, living areas, dusting, vacuuming, mopping, bins, supplied products and equipment, laundry, ironing, and selected appliance interiors.
- Add a prominent, scannable “What’s included” summary before package selection, while retaining detailed room-by-room information for customers who want it.
- Use calm CTAs such as **Book This Clean** and keep prices visible without extra clicks.

## Pricing Communication
- Lead with **“One clear price. No hourly calculations.”**
- Explain that the price is based on the home and chosen service and is known before arrival.
- Keep supporting messages about supplied products and equipment, without discount or competitor language.

## Laundry
- Present Small, Medium, and Large loads as clear selectable rows with maximum weights and visible prices.
- Keep the confirmed two fulfilment options: on-site wash/dry/iron and collection/return.
- Show washing, drying, folding or ironing, detergent status, and collection/delivery status in one restrained service summary.
- Do not invent detergent inclusion: label it transparently as **“Please confirm when booking”** until the business supplies the policy.
- Keep the confirmed 24–48 hour turnaround and existing prices and booking flow.

## Technical Details
- Update the existing static service markup, design-system CSS, and pricing/booking script only.
- Preserve package IDs and pricing values so existing booking summaries and WhatsApp/email requests continue working.
- Extend the current organic reveal animation selectors to the new comparison and inclusion elements.
- Verify desktop and mobile layouts, package toggling, laundry selections, visible price updates, and booking launches.
