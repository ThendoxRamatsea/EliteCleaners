# Final professional polish for Elite Cleaners

## What will change
- Unify heading scale, text widths, section spacing, button proportions, image corners, shadows, and alignment while preserving the approved navy, gold, Lora, and Nunito Sans direction.
- Reduce repetition by removing the least useful standalone booking prompt and the public “reviews coming soon” block, while keeping booking available at the header, hero, pricing, recurring care, final section, footer, and mobile bar.
- Simplify the main navigation to Home, Home Cleaning, Laundry, About, FAQs, and Contact; keep Corporate and Deep Cleaning discoverable in the services area and footer.
- Refine the service overview, package comparison, pricing, laundry selector, team placeholders, trust section, and forms without replacing their existing structure.
- Simplify the footer to real, useful destinations only: branding, primary navigation, services, contact details, and genuine social/legal links when available. Placeholder social and legal links will be removed rather than presented as active.

## Mobile polish
- Tune the hero heading, image height, and spacing for small phones without making the page feel compressed.
- Reduce oversized tablet spacing and improve the service comparison, price cards, laundry rows, and booking form at 390px and 734px widths.
- Preserve clear 44px-or-larger touch targets, prevent horizontal scrolling, improve long booking-summary wrapping, and keep the mobile booking bar clear of footer content.
- Keep the mobile menu contained below the header and make its open/close state clearer.

## Interaction, accessibility, and performance
- Limit scroll reveals to meaningful groups, shorten their travel and timing, and keep reduced-motion support.
- Restrain hover movement and shadows so the interface feels quiet and stable.
- Add stronger keyboard focus treatment, clearer menu and selection states, and complete accessible labels/states for interactive controls.
- Replace script-dependent placeholder contact links with working WhatsApp/contact links and add explicit image dimensions where missing.
- Keep existing compressed photography and lazy loading; avoid heavy visual effects or unnecessary new assets.

## Verification
- Review the full page at desktop, 734px tablet, and 390px phone widths.
- Test navigation, package switching, detailed inclusions, laundry pricing, corporate form layout, booking steps, modal keyboard behavior, sticky booking action, and footer links.
- Confirm no horizontal overflow, console errors, broken links, hidden content, or blocked booking paths.
