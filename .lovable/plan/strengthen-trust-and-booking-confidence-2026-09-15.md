# Strengthen Trust and Booking Confidence

## Goal
Make Elite Cleaners feel professional, reliable, and accountable without changing the existing editorial navy-and-gold design or adding visual clutter.

## What will change
- Replace the current general “Why Elite” area with a refined **The Elite Standard** section using only supported facts: professional Ace of Maids training, careful selection, a shared service standard, respect for the home, and supplied products and equipment.
- Add **Your home, consistently cared for** with restrained Weekly, Fortnightly, and Once-Off choices. No discounts or new prices will be implied.
- Simplify **How it works** to the requested three steps: choose your clean, pick your date, and let Elite take care of the rest.
- Standardise the meaningful booking buttons to **Book Your Clean** in the header, hero, after services, after home-cleaning pricing, near the bottom, footer, and mobile booking bar. Service-specific buttons such as **Book This Clean** and corporate quote actions will remain specific.
- Recompose the final call-to-action as **Come home to clean**, using an existing clean-home photograph and one calm primary booking action.

## Reviews and Guarantee
- Add a restrained reviews area that does not fabricate customer feedback. Until real reviews are supplied, it will clearly say that verified feedback will be added rather than showing invented names or quotes.
- Build **The Elite Clean Guarantee** with the exact proposed wording, but keep it disabled by default behind one clearly named setting until the business confirms the policy.

## Technical details
- Update only the existing page markup, design-system CSS, and booking script.
- Preserve all current pricing, service data, image assets, booking behaviour, WhatsApp/email flows, and organic scroll animation style.
- Verify desktop and mobile layouts, all booking entry points, the three-step section, recurring-care choices, and the hidden-by-default guarantee.
